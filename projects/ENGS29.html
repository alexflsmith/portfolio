Dino Fashion Show

Developed a kinematic walking path and subsequent 6-bar linkage for a robotic dinosaur.

Duration
March 2025 - May 2025
Type
Individual
Tools
SolidWorks · 3D Printing · Laser Cutting

Overview

Tasked with creating a 6 bar walking path for a robot. Took inspiration from previous designs and created the most compact linkage in the class while maintaining a steady walking motion.

Responsibilities

  1. Designed 6 bar linkage sketch using a crank and crank extension, coupler, rocker, strut, and translator
  2. Built out the sketch in SolidWorks while maintaining low profile design and functionality
  3. Laser cut or 3D printed parts and assembled to finalize build

Iteration

Geometric design Superseded

  • Basic 6 bar kinematic design of walking path using Geogebra

SolidWorks Motion Study Current

  • Adaptation of Geogebra geometry into SolidWorks for Motion Study Analysis

Final Design

Final assembly design after geometric iteration and design of individual components
